  • Sheet provenance is July 2026; the effective signed/approved construction issue has not been established.
  • Actual beam-to-column load paths, wall alignment, engineer-approved rebar schedules, stirrup zone lengths and temporary propping are separate checks.
  • Original sheets do not authorize construction, fabrication quantities, concrete placement or completion-status changes.
