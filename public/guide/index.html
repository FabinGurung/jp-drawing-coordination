How to use the drawings

  1. Open your project from Projects.
  2. Choose a drawing version and floor.
  3. Switch between the 2D plan and 3D model, or show both.
  4. Select a wall, door or other drawing item to inspect its location and dimensions.
  5. Open Measurements or Documents for available tables and drawings.
Drawings and measurements are not construction approval. Survey, structural review and statutory approvals remain separate.
